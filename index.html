IneSight

Capstone project

A web-based community health monitoring and decision support system for Barangay Santa Ines, Plaridel, Bulacan. It helps barangay health personnel monitor community health information and support decisions through a purok-level map. Each purok is shaded on the map using GeoJSON purok boundaries.

The system also includes charts, searchable data tables and PDF report generation. I'm working with my team on designing and developing it.
